Math words

Probability
How likely something is. A coin lands heads 1 out of 2 times, so the probability is 50%.
Payout
How much a win pays. “Pays 2 to 1” means you win 2 credits for each 1 credit you bet, and you keep your bet.
Fair payout
The payout that makes a game even, so nobody wins or loses in the long run.
Expected value
Your average win or loss per bet if you played forever. It is usually a negative number in a casino.
House edge
The part of every bet the casino keeps on average. A 5% edge means the casino keeps about 5 of every 100 credits bet.
Return
The part of every bet that comes back to players on average. Return = 100% minus the house edge.
Luck (variance)
How far results swing away from the average in the short run. Big prizes that rarely hit mean big swings.
Law of large numbers
The more rounds you play, the closer your results get to the expected value. Luck fades. Math stays.
Commission
A small fee the house takes out of winning bets. It turns a fair game into a game the house wins.
